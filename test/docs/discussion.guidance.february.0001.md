# February stakeholder and architecture review

On February 15, Morgan withdrew the preference for batch delivery for enterprise
customers and favored streaming delivery for them instead. Morgan explicitly
retained batch delivery for small-account customers and opposition to weekend
support.

Architect Casey assessed the shared reference interface as feasible, conditional
on completing the tenant-isolation review. Casey's stated basis was the prototype
exercise. Casey recommended a sandbox trial after that review. The meeting did
not approve the interface or authorize the trial. The review remains unresolved.
